C++ Console Application

Spear Me

Spear Me is a Trojan War battle game where the player chooses a fighter, picks a weapon, and starts a battle. The player chooses the type of attack they want to use and plays against a randomized computer opponent. Their wins and losses are saved into the game so they can track their record over time.

Key Features

  • Choose a fighter from one of two teams, so picking a fighter also means picking a side.
  • Player-choice combat where you pick a normal or critical attack each turn, with critical dealing double damage but risking a miss.
  • A save and load record that tracks wins and losses, motivating players to keep playing and maintain a winning score.

What I Learned

While testing my code, I discovered a bug where a character's health was not resetting. If you chose the same character, or the computer randomly picked one you'd already fought, they still had damage from the last battle and would die instantly. I wanted players to be able to use characters again in new battles, so I created a max health value and reset it after every battle. This taught me how to track down a bug through testing.
